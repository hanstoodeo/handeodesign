Smart
Mirror
Granted 18 January 2022 · Application No. 29/733,908
A freestanding smart mirror conceived as a standalone product — an ambient display that lives in the spaces where people already pause: the bedroom, the hallway, the dressing room.

The design patent protects the ornamental form itself: a near-frameless reflective surface with a single vertical seam, mounted on a weighted base. The restraint is deliberate — the object needs to disappear into a room until it's needed, then surface information without breaking the environment it's in.
