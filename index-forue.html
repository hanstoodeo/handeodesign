About

Han Min Lee

Design as a discipline, not a deliverable.

I'm a product designer with 20+ years leading digital experience at brands where the standard is genuinely high — Samsung, Dyson, and now Entain. I work at the intersection of editorial craft, design systems, and user research, building products that hold quality at scale.

I've led teams of up to 25, secured international patents, and contributed to work that's won IDEA Gold and multiple iF Awards. I'm drawn to products where design decisions carry cultural weight — where there's real craft to protect and push forward.
